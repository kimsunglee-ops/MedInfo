## 1. Module learning outcomes

| MLO | Module learning outcome |
|---|---|
| **MLO1** | Explain and interpret healthcare data, including data types, lifecycle, quality, context and terminology, in biomedical and healthcare contexts. |
| **MLO2** | Analyse how healthcare information systems and interoperability enable the generation, management and exchange of reliable laboratory and clinical information. |
| **MLO3** | Apply appropriate analytical and visualisation approaches to healthcare data and evaluate analytical, decision-support and model-generated outputs for healthcare decision-making, including their limitations. |
| **MLO4** | Evaluate digital-health technologies, including AI-enabled tools, for clinical and biomedical applications, considering validity, safety, usability, bias, regulatory status and fitness for purpose. |
| **MLO5** | Analyse privacy, cybersecurity, ethical, legal and governance requirements associated with healthcare data and digital technologies, and propose appropriate risk controls. |
| **MLO6** | Integrate medical informatics concepts to evaluate or design a BMS-relevant healthcare solution or workflow and communicate its rationale, benefits, risks and trade-offs. |

**D2 rule:** MLO3 = judgement about **outputs** (chart, dashboard, alert, AI interpretation). MLO4 = judgement about **technologies** (device, app, AI tool) as fit for purpose.

**Compound MLOs:** MLO3, MLO4 and MLO5 each contain two actions. If NP rules permit, assess each component with a separate rubric criterion.

**MLO vs WLO:** MLOs are end-of-module capabilities; weekly learning outcomes (WLOs) are week-level. They are not interchangeable.
