## 1. Module learning outcomes

On successful completion, students will be able to:

| MLO | Outcome | Bloom level | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| MLO1 | **Explain** how health information is generated, flows and is used across Singapore's healthcare ecosystem to support clinical, laboratory and population health decisions. | Understand | A |
| MLO2 | **Evaluate** the quality, standardisation and fitness-for-purpose of clinical and laboratory data for a stated healthcare use. | Evaluate | A–B |
| MLO3 | **Analyse** how healthcare information systems (including EHR, LIS and decision support) support or constrain biomedical and clinical workflows and patient safety. | Analyse | B–C |
| MLO4 | **Interpret** health data analyses and AI model outputs, including performance metrics and limitations, to inform healthcare decisions. | Apply / Analyse | C |
| MLO5 | **Evaluate** the ethical, legal, privacy and governance implications of using health data and AI, with reference to Singapore's regulatory context. | Evaluate | D |
| MLO6 | **Justify** an evidence-based recommendation on the adoption or improvement of a digital health solution, integrating stakeholder needs, risks and responsible GenAI use. | Evaluate / Create | D |

## 4. Writing rules for weekly LOs

- 2–4 per week; each maps to ≥1 MLO.
- Measurable verbs only: explain, describe, interpret, apply, compare, analyse, evaluate, justify, recommend, design. **Never "understand", "know", "appreciate".**
- Each LO names an observable piece of evidence (see `Weekly_Syllabus.md`).
- Cognitive level must not exceed what the week's practical lets students demonstrate.
