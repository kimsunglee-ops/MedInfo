Let me introduce Patient A. Patient A is a fictional adult with type 2 diabetes, seen today in an outpatient clinic. The doctor has ordered a renal panel, which includes potassium, and an HbA1c.

Notice what we are not going to do. We are not going to interpret Patient A's results, and we are not making a diagnosis. That is not our role here. Our job this week is to follow the information around the test.
