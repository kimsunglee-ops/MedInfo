Welcome to Medical Informatics. Over the next ten weeks we will look at how healthcare data are created, checked, shared and used — and you will see that laboratories sit right at the centre of that story.

This week we start with something you already know well: a blood test. You have handled specimens and run analysers in your laboratory modules. What we are adding now is the information that travels with the specimen and with the result.

Here is the question I want you to keep in mind: when an analyser produces a number, why is that not yet a reliable result in the doctor's hands? By the end of the lesson you should be able to answer it from several angles.

Everything in this module is fictional — the patients, the hospital and the results — so we can examine problems openly without touching anyone's real data.
