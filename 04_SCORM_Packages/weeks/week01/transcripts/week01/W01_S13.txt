We'll use three words for information risk throughout the module: error, delay and loss.

An error means the information is wrong — for example, a correct value filed against the wrong patient.

A delay means the right information arrives, but too late to support the decision it was needed for.

A loss means the information is missing, or never reaches anyone who can act on it.

In the practical you'll mark these on your map with three symbols: a triangle for error, a clock for delay and a broken circle for loss. They differ by shape, so they work even without colour.
